# Blade Toss — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Throw knives into the spinning log — but never hit one that's already
stuck. Fill the meter to burst the log and advance, snag apples for +5,
and face a big tricky boss log every 5th level. One tap to play, hard
to master.

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Timing
- Controls: Mouse click / Touch tap. Keyboard: Space or ↑ to throw.
- Features: Endless level progression with tightening hit windows,
  random speed pulses and direction flips, apple bonuses (+5), boss
  levels every 5th level, hit-stop juice, wood-chip particle bursts,
  best score & best level saving, procedural graphics & sounds,
  single-file HTML5 (no external assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking.
